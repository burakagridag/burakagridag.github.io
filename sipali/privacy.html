In short

Sipali is a personal expense and budget tracker that works entirely on your device. Your financial data — accounts, transactions, budgets — is stored encrypted on your phone and is never sent to us. We run no server, there are no user accounts and there is no sign-up.

The only point at which data leaves your device is the advertising shown in the app, which is served by Google AdMob.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter stays on your device:

  • Accounts and their balances, transactions (amount, date, category, account, note), categories, budgets, recurring transactions and saved templates
  • Settings such as language, currency, theme and whether balances are hidden
  • Your passcode, stored only as a salted hash in the system's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) — the passcode itself is never stored

The database is encrypted (SQLCipher); its key is kept in the system's secure storage. This data never leaves the device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in system settings.

Face ID / fingerprint

If you turn on biometric unlock, the check is performed by your device's operating system. The app only receives a "success" or "failure" result — it never sees or stores your face or fingerprint data.

Reminders

Reminders for recurring transactions are local notifications scheduled on your device. No notification service or server is involved.

Backups and exports

Backups (.mwbak) and CSV / Excel exports are created only when you tap the button, and saved or shared wherever you choose. Backup and export files are not encrypted — keep them somewhere you trust. We never receive them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. In doing so, Google may process personal data, in particular:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, device type, operating system version and language
  • interactions with the ad (impressions, clicks)

Your financial data is never shared with Google or any advertiser. Google is the controller for the ad processing. See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services for details.

Consent

Before the first ad is requested, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland). If you decline personalised advertising, only non-personalised ads are served. You can change your choice at any time through the same form.

On iOS the system additionally asks for permission via App Tracking Transparency before the advertising ID may be used for cross-app tracking.

Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) for personalised advertising, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in funding the app) for non-personalised advertising.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking services, no crash reporting
  • No user accounts, no registration, no email collection
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties beyond the ad delivery described above
  • No access to contacts, location, camera or microphone

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your data, as well as the rights to object and to data portability. Since we store no personal data ourselves, please direct requests about the advertising processing to Google; for anything else, contact us at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
