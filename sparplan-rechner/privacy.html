In short

Sparplan-Rechner calculates entirely on your device. Everything you enter — amounts, expected return, duration and tax settings — is stored locally and is never sent to us. We run no server, there are no user accounts and there is no sign-up.

The only point at which data leaves your device is the advertisement shown in the app, which is served by Google AdMob.

Data stored on your device

So the app resumes where you left off, it stores the following locally using the system's app-preferences storage:

  • Language, currency and the assumed inflation rate
  • Tax settings: country, filing status, church tax and a tax rate you entered yourself, if any
  • The last-used inputs of both calculators (starting amount, monthly amount, expected return, duration, target amount)

This data never leaves the device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data in system settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows one banner on the calculator screen, served by Google AdMob. In doing so, Google may process personal data, in particular:

  • your device's advertising ID
  • IP address, device type, operating system version and language
  • interactions with the ad (impressions, clicks)

Google is the controller for this processing. See the Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob and privacy for details and the list of partners involved.

Consent

Before the first ad is requested, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). If you decline personalised advertising, only non-personalised ads are served. You can change your choice at any time through the same form.

On iOS the system additionally asks for permission via App Tracking Transparency before the advertising ID may be used for cross-app tracking.

Legal bases: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) for personalised advertising, and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in funding the app) for non-personalised advertising.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking services (no Firebase Analytics, no crash reporting)
  • No user accounts, no registration, no email collection
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties beyond the ad delivery described above
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone or files
